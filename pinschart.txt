Raspberry PI finalized pins
---------------------------

-- Components connected to the RPI: --

Servo motor for steering:
Vcc 5V, GND, Signal

Encoder on right rear wheel:
Vcc 3.3V, GND, DT, CLK

Encoder on left rear wheel:
Vcc 3.3V, GND, DT, CLK

Potentiometer as encoder for steering:
Vcc 3.3V, GND, D0

Color sensor:
S0, S1, S2, S3, OUT, Vcc 5V, GND

4 distance sensors:
SCL, SDA, GND, Vcc 3.3V, XSHUT (each sensor needs its own XSHUT GPIO)

Motor driver:
IN1 – digital GPIO (signal to control direction), IN2 – digital GPIO (signal to control direction), ENA – PWM-capable GPIO (for motor speed control)

IMU sensor:
SCL, SDA, Vcc 3.3V or 5V, GND

Push activation button:
Signal, GND

-------------------------

GPIO 2 (Pin 3) – Connected to SDA line for I2C.
This is shared by the IMU sensor and the 4 VL53L0X distance sensors.

GPIO 3 (Pin 5) – Connected to SCL line for I2C.
This is also shared by the IMU and the 4 VL53L0X sensors.

GPIO 4 (Pin 7) – Connected to XSHUT of VL53L0X sensor 1.

GPIO 18 (Pin 12) – Connected to XSHUT of VL53L0X sensor 2.

GPIO 25 (Pin 22) – Connected to XSHUT of VL53L0X sensor 3.

GPIO 8 (Pin 24) – Connected to XSHUT of VL53L0X sensor 4.

GPIO 12 (Pin 32) – Connected to the servo motor for steering (PWM signal).

GPIO 21 (Pin 40) – Connected to ENA pin of the motor driver (PWM speed control).

GPIO 14 (Pin 8) – Connected to IN1 of the motor driver (direction signal).

GPIO 15 (Pin 10) – Connected to IN2 of the motor driver (direction signal).

GPIO 5 (Pin 29) – Connected to DT of the right rear encoder.

GPIO 6 (Pin 31) – Connected to CLK of the right rear encoder.

GPIO 13 (Pin 33) – Connected to DT of the left rear encoder.

GPIO 19 (Pin 35) – Connected to CLK of the left rear encoder.

GPIO 26 (Pin 37) – Connected to the D0 signal from the potentiometer used as steering encoder.

GPIO 17 (Pin 11) – Connected to S0 of the color sensor.

GPIO 27 (Pin 13) – Connected to S1 of the color sensor.

GPIO 22 (Pin 15) – Connected to S2 of the color sensor.

GPIO 23 (Pin 16) – Connected to S3 of the color sensor.

GPIO 24 (Pin 18) – Connected to OUT signal of the color sensor.

GPIO 16 (Pin 36) – Connected to push button (start button).
