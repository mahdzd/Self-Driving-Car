Raspberry PI finalized pins
---------------------------

-- Components connected to the RPI: --

Servo motor for steering:
Vcc 5V, GND, Signal

Encoder on right rear wheel:
Vcc 3.3V, GND, DT, CLK

Encoder on left rear wheel:
Vcc 3.3V, GND, DT, CLK

Potentiometer as encoder for steering:
Vcc 3.3V, GND, D0

Color sensor:
S0, S1, S2, S3, OUT, Vcc 5V, GND

4 distance sensors:
SCL, SDA, GND, Vcc 3.3V, XSHUT (each sensor needs its own XSHUT GPIO)

Motor driver:
IN1 – digital GPIO (signal to control direction), IN2 – digital GPIO (signal to control direction), ENA – PWM-capable GPIO (for motor speed control)

IMU sensor:
SCL, SDA, Vcc 3.3V or 5V, GND

Push activation button:
Signal, GND

-------------------------

GPIO 2 (Pin 3) – SDA line for I2C (shared by IMU & 4 VL53L0X sensors)

GPIO 3 (Pin 5) – SCL line for I2C (shared by IMU & 4 VL53L0X sensors)

GPIO 4 (Pin 7) – XSHUT of VL53L0X sensor 1

GPIO 5 (Pin 29) – DT of rotary encoder

GPIO 6 (Pin 31) – CLK of rotary encoder

GPIO 10 (Pin 19) – Right encoder D0

GPIO 12 (Pin 32) – Servo motor for steering (PWM)

GPIO 14 (Pin 8) – IN1 of motor driver (direction)

GPIO 15 (Pin 10) – IN2 of motor driver (direction)

GPIO 16 (Pin 36) – Push button (start)

GPIO 17 (Pin 11) – S0 of color sensor

GPIO 18 (Pin 12) – XSHUT of VL53L0X sensor 2

GPIO 21 (Pin 40) – ENA of motor driver (PWM speed)

GPIO 22 (Pin 15) – S2 of color sensor

GPIO 23 (Pin 16) – S3 of color sensor

GPIO 24 (Pin 18) – XSHUT of VL53L0X sensor 3

GPIO 25 (Pin 22) – XSHUT of VL53L0X sensor 4

GPIO 26 (Pin 37) – Left encoder D0 

GPIO 27 (Pin 13) – S1 of color sensor
